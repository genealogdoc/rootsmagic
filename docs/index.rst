Understanding the RootsMagic Login Account
------------------------------------------

Your RootsMagic login account is not a single credential but a collection of different accounts that serve distinct purposes. The software uses a license key system for software registration, a separate MyRootsMagic account for publishing trees online, and connections to external services like FamilySearch and Ancestry that require their own login credentials.

This multi-layered approach gives you flexibility and control over your data while allowing you to benefit from online resources. Your RootsMagic login account for cloud publishing is completely free and enables you to share your family tree with relatives without any ongoing subscription costs.

Creating Your MyRootsMagic Account
----------------------------------

The MyRootsMagic account is one of the most valuable features accessible through your RootsMagic login account. This free service allows you to publish your family tree online for others to view. To get started, you open RootsMagic, select the Publish page, and then choose MyRootsMagic from the menu options [citation:4].

Click the "Sign In or Create Account" button to begin the process. If you have not created an account yet, click the "Create an account" link and enter your email address and a desired username [citation:3]. RootsMagic will create the account and then let you log in. Once you have logged in, you will see a list of any trees you have previously published.

After logging in to your RootsMagic login account for MyRootsMagic, you can publish a new tree, view any existing tree, change settings such as title and description, refresh the data for your tree by reuploading your database, or delete your tree [citation:4]. The Settings button also allows you to change your email or password, giving you complete control over your account.

Registering Your Software License
---------------------------------

When you install RootsMagic on your computer, you need to enter your registration key to unlock the full version. This is another aspect of your RootsMagic login account experience, though it involves a license key rather than a traditional username and password.

To enter your registration key, open RootsMagic and select "Unlock Full Registered Version" on the welcome screen [citation:1]. Enter your name and registration key exactly as provided. The key is case sensitive and must be entered with all uppercase letters, including the dashes, without any spacing in the sequence [citation:1]. Once the key has been entered, the "Unlock" button will highlight. Click it to proceed.

The next screen gives you the option to register your software. You can register online, by phone, or later. Registering online is recommended as it connects your RootsMagic login account to your license. If you have lost your registration key, you can request it to be sent to your email address by visiting the recovery page or calling customer support [citation:1].

Connecting to FamilySearch and Ancestry
---------------------------------------

RootsMagic integrates with major genealogy databases, allowing you to access billions of records and synchronize your tree. However, this requires additional login credentials beyond your basic RootsMagic login account. You need to use your existing FamilySearch or Ancestry accounts to connect to these services through the software.

A critical point to understand is that older versions of RootsMagic may no longer work with these external services. In December 2023, Ancestry and FamilySearch turned off their old login procedures used in RootsMagic versions 7 and 8 [citation:6]. Only RootsMagic version 9.1 or higher supports the new OAuth technology that enables two-step verification [citation:6]. If you are using an older version and cannot log in to FamilySearch or Ancestry, upgrading to the latest version is required.

If you experience login issues with FamilySearch, there are several troubleshooting steps to try. In a main view, use the key combination Ctrl+Shift+U and select "Reset Program Settings" [citation:2]. Close and reopen the software, then go to Publish and FamilySearch Central to see if you can log in. You may also need to temporarily turn off your firewall, antivirus, and internet filtering software to determine if one is blocking access [citation:2].

For Ancestry TreeShare login issues, the same reset procedure can help. If you are using two-factor verification, you must append the verification code to the end of your password without any spaces when logging in through the software [citation:2]. This is a common compatibility issue between RootsMagic and two-factor authentication systems.

Troubleshooting Your RootsMagic Login Account
---------------------------------------------

Various issues can arise when accessing different components of your RootsMagic login account. For MyRootsMagic publishing, ensure you are using the correct email address and password. If you have lost your credentials, use the account recovery options on the login screen.

If you are migrating from an older version of RootsMagic, you may need to transfer your MyRootsMagic website. In RootsMagic 7 or earlier, go to Internet and MyRootsMagic. Click the Settings button at the top and select "Sign into MyRootsMagic online" [citation:8]. Near the bottom of the page, click the link "Where did my site(s) go?" to transfer any RM6 sites [citation:8]. Once transferred, you can delete them and republish using the newer version.

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

**Question: How do I create a RootsMagic login account for publishing my tree online?**

You create a MyRootsMagic account from within the software. Select the Publish page, choose MyRootsMagic, and click "Create an account." Enter your email and desired login to set up your free account .

**Question: Why can I not log in to FamilySearch or Ancestry from my version of RootsMagic?**

Older versions of RootsMagic (version 8 and below) no longer support the login procedures for FamilySearch and Ancestry. You need to upgrade to RootsMagic version 9.1 or higher to connect to these services .

**Question: How do I enter my registration key for RootsMagic?**

Open RootsMagic and select "Unlock Full Registered Version" on the welcome screen. Enter your name and registration key exactly as provided. The key is case sensitive and requires dashes .

**Question: What should I do if I forgot my RootsMagic registration key?**

You can request your registration key to be sent to your email address by visiting the recovery page. Use the email address you used when purchasing the software . You can also call customer support for assistance .

**Question: What is MyRootsMagic and how do I use it?**

MyRootsMagic is a free service that lets you publish your family tree online. You can access it through the Publish page in the software. Your RootsMagic login account for MyRootsMagic allows you to publish, view, and manage your online tree .

**Question: Can I use two-factor verification with RootsMagic?**

Two-factor verification works with Ancestry but requires appending the verification code to your password when logging in through RootsMagic. FamilySearch's login system does not currently support two-factor verification .
